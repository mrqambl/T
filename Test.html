Rules: use the past simple · use at least 3 kinds of time markers · use linking words instead of “;” · write a topic sentence and a concluding sentence · explain 2 of the ideas a little bit.

A. Your skeleton (easy template)

1. Topic sentence (start)
  • [Name] was a [job] from [country].
  • [Name] had a very interesting life.
2. Time markers (when?)
  • In 1955, In 1977,
  • Then, After that, Later,
  • Two years later, Between 1995 and 2000,
  • At first, Finally, In the end,
3. Linking words (join ideas)
  • and = add an idea
  • but / however = a different idea
  • so = result
  • because = reason
  • then / after that = next
4. Explain an idea (2 times)
  • She/He did this because ...
  • This was hard, so she/he ...
5. Concluding sentence (end)
  • In conclusion, [Name] was a ... person.
  • Her/His life shows that ...
Quick steps: ① topic sentence → ② events in order with time markers → ③ explain 2 events → ④ concluding sentence → ⑤ check: past tense? 3 time markers? 80+ words?

Aisha Al-Marri was a successful businesswoman from Qatar. She was born in Doha in 1955. In 1977, she finished university in Australia, but she didn’t find a job, so she started a small home fashion business at home. Eight years later, she opened her first clothes shop and hired two workers. In 1988, she had money problems, but she didn’t close her business because she believed in her dream. Instead, she worked harder and saved money. Between 1995 and 2000, she grew the business and opened more shops. In 2016, she retired, and her daughter took over the business. Finally, she died in Doha in 2025. In conclusion, Aisha was a strong woman who never gave up.

(Ideas explained: no job → she started her own business; money problems → she worked harder. About 115 words.)
