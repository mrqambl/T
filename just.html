  • Use linking words to replace semicolons (;) and slashes (/).
  • Write a topic sentence and a concluding sentence.

Writing Skeleton / Template (Easy Words):

[Name] was a famous [job/profession]. He/She was born in [Year] in [Place]. In [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. Later, in [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. During the [Years/Decade], he/she [past tense verb + event]. In [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. Finally, in [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. He/She had a very [adjective] life and will always be remembered.

Full Written Answer (Aisha Al-Marri):

Aisha Al-Marri was a successful businesswoman. She was born in 1955 in Doha, Qatar. In 1977, she finished university in Australia, but she didn't find a job. So, she started a small home fashion business. In 1985, she opened her first clothes shop and hired two workers. In 1988, she had money problems, but she didn't close her business. During the 1990s, she grew her business and opened more shops. In 2016, she retired and her daughter took over the business. Finally, she died in Doha in 2025. She had a very inspiring life and will always be remembered.

Writing Skeleton / Template (Easy Words):

[Name] was a famous [job/profession]. He/She was born in [Year] in [Place]. In [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. In [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event] and [past tense verb + event]. Later, in [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. In [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. Finally, in [Year], he/she [past tense verb + event]. He/She had a very [adjective] life and will always be remembered.

Full Written Answer (Raul Khan):

Raul Khan was a famous actor. He was born in 1942 in India. In 1969, he acted in his first movie. In 1973, he became famous with 'Life', his first successful film, and he got married and started a family. In 1982, he worked with other famous actors. Later, in 1991, he made two comedy films. In 2001, he won the 'Best Actor' Award in India. In 2002, he became very rich, but he lived a very simple life. Finally, in 2019, he gave a lot of money to poor farmers. He had a very wonderful life and will always be remembered.
